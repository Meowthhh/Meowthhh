## About me  <img src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/egonelbre/gophers/master/.thumb/animation/gopher-dance-long-3x.gif' width='20px' alt='Assalamu Alaikum'>

<p align='justify'>
I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from <a href='https://www.iutoic-dhaka.edu/'>Islamic University of Technology (IUT)</a>, with a strong and growing focus on research in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. My current interests include Medical AI, Computer Vision, Foundation Models, Few-Shot Learning, Cross-Dataset Generalization, Explainable AI, robustness, and multilingual NLP. I am especially interested in designing careful experiments that go beyond standard in-domain accuracy and help us understand how well AI systems generalize, explain their decisions, and remain reliable across datasets and domains.

Alongside research, I have a strong background in problem-solving and software development, having solved thousands of programming problems across different online platforms. I value readable, reusable, and reproducible code, and I enjoy turning research questions into practical experimental pipelines. I am continuously learning modern deep-learning methods, research methodologies, and tools for building rigorous and reproducible AI systems.
</p>
