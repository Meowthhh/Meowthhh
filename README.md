## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Science & Engineering graduate from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT)** with interests spanning **machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and software development**.

My current work is increasingly research-focused. I am particularly interested in building and evaluating ML systems that are **robust, explainable, reproducible, and useful beyond a single dataset**. I also enjoy solving algorithmic problems and turning research ideas into practical implementations.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- Medical image classification and disease detection
- Cross-dataset and cross-domain generalization
- Computer vision, CNNs, Vision Transformers, and foundation models
- Explainable AI (XAI), robustness, and model evaluation
- NLP and LLM evaluation, including Bangla and multilingual settings
- Efficient experimentation under limited compute resources
